### Vlad-Alexandru Vasiu

M.Sc. Mathematics in Data Science student at the Technical University of Munich. B.Sc. graduate in both Mathematics and Computer Science from Babeș-Bolyai University, Cluj-Napoca.

I work on statistical modelling and machine learning for noisy real-world time series: estimating stochastic processes, testing whether data actually satisfies a model's assumptions, and producing uncertainty estimates that hold up when checked.

**Selected projects**

- **[StochEstimate](https://github.com/03vladd/StochEstimate)**: robust Ornstein-Uhlenbeck parameter estimation for financial spreads. Compares Gaussian MLE, Student-t MLE and LSTM estimators under jump contamination, gates real equity spreads through a statistical validation battery, and replaces overconfident MC Dropout intervals with split-conformal ones (93.5 to 99% coverage). Bachelor's thesis, with a companion paper.
- **[Euroleague-Fantasy](https://github.com/03vladd/Euroleague-Fantasy)**: XGBoost forecasts of player performance from 35 leakage-safe features, plus mixed-integer programming for squad selection. A walk-forward backtest over the 2025-26 season beat a rolling-average baseline by 28%.
- **QuantifiedStrides** (private, demo on request): athlete performance analytics built on wearable sensor data. Includes an ETL pipeline, fitness-fatigue and HRV recovery models, retrieval over coaching sources with sentence embeddings and pgvector, and a contextual-bandit recommender.
